Why are sunrise colours so vivid?

Sunlight travels through more atmosphere near the horizon.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerSunlight travels through more atmosphere near the horizon.

What is happening?

When the Sun is low, shorter blue wavelengths scatter out of the direct beam, leaving warmer reds, pinks and oranges.

Why does it matter?

Clouds, haze and dust change how colours appear; pollution may intensify colour while harming air quality.
