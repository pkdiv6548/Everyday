Why are flamingos pink?

Pigments in their food gradually colour their feathers.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerPigments in their food gradually colour their feathers.

What is happening?

Flamingos eat algae and small crustaceans containing carotenoid pigments. Their bodies process these compounds and deposit pigments in feathers and skin.

Why does it matter?

Young flamingos are often greyish before developing their adult colour. Diet and health influence colour intensity.
