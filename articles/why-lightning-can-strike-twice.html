Can lightning strike the same place twice?

Yes. Tall or exposed structures can be struck repeatedly.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerYes. Tall or exposed structures can be struck repeatedly.

What is happening?

Lightning follows an electrical path between cloud and ground. Towers, tall buildings and ridgelines can be favoured paths, so a previous strike does not make a location safe.

Why does it matter?

During a storm, shelter in a substantial building or hard-topped vehicle. Stay away from windows, plumbing and wired electronics.
