Why is static electricity worse in winter?

Dry air lets electric charge remain on surfaces for longer.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerDry air lets electric charge remain on surfaces for longer.

What is happening?

In low humidity, thin moisture films on surfaces are reduced, so charge leaks away more slowly. Friction between shoes, clothes and carpets can build up charge until it discharges in a small shock.

Why does it matter?

A humidifier and natural-fibre clothing may reduce static buildup indoors.
