Why does wood usually float?

Many kinds of wood have an average density lower than water.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerMany kinds of wood have an average density lower than water.

What is happening?

Wood contains cell walls and tiny spaces that can hold air. For many species, the combined density is below water’s density, so wood floats.

Why does it matter?

Some dense woods sink, and waterlogged wood may lose buoyancy as pores fill with water.
