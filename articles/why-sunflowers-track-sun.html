Do sunflowers follow the Sun?

Young sunflowers can track sunlight through daily growth.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerYoung sunflowers can track sunlight through daily growth.

What is happening?

Growth differs across the stem during the day, turning young plants toward sunlight. Mature sunflower heads often stop tracking and face east.

Why does it matter?

East-facing mature flowers warm early and may help attract pollinators.
