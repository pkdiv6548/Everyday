Why does water expand when it freezes?

Hydrogen bonds form an open crystal structure in ice.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerHydrogen bonds form an open crystal structure in ice.

What is happening?

As water freezes, hydrogen bonds arrange molecules into a structure that takes more space than liquid water, making ice less dense.

Why does it matter?

Leave room in rigid containers before freezing water. Expansion can crack containers, especially glass.
