Does a mirror really reverse left and right?

A mirror flips front and back, not left and right in the way we imagine.

5 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerRaise your right hand in front of a mirror and the reflection appears to raise the hand on your left. It feels like left and right have swapped, but the geometry is more subtle.

The mirror changes depth

A flat mirror reflects light so that the image appears the same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front. The direction pointing toward the mirror is reversed. Left and right are not swapped by the mirror itself; we mentally compare the image with a person turned around to face us.

Try a simple label test

Write a word on a transparent sheet and hold it in front of a mirror. Then turn the sheet around and compare the result. You will see that the mirror reflects the front-to-back orientation, while turning the sheet changes which side faces the mirror.

Try a safe mini experiment

Use a small mirror and paper letters. Keep glass mirrors away from edges where they could fall; a lightweight unbreakable mirror is safer for children.
