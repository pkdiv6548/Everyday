Why do rainbows look like arcs?

Each raindrop sends coloured light toward your eyes at a particular angle.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerEach raindrop sends coloured light toward your eyes at a particular angle.

What is happening?

A rainbow forms when sunlight refracts, reflects inside droplets and refracts again as it exits. The geometry sends the brightest coloured light toward an observer at a characteristic angle.

Why does it matter?

A full circle can form from an aircraft or high viewpoint, but the ground usually blocks the lower half from a person standing on Earth.

Try noticing itLook for this effect in everyday life, compare what changes, and remember that a simple observation is the beginning of a scientific question.
