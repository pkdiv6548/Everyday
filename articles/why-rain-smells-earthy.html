Why does rain smell earthy?

Raindrops can release soil compounds and plant oils into the air.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerRaindrops can release soil compounds and plant oils into the air.

What is happening?

The smell after rain is often called petrichor. Raindrops can trap tiny air bubbles that burst and carry airborne particles, including compounds made by soil microbes.

Why does it matter?

The scent varies with soil, plants, weather and how long the ground was dry.
