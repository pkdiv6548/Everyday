Why do we get goosebumps?

Tiny muscles at hair follicles contract in response to cold or emotion.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerTiny muscles at hair follicles contract in response to cold or emotion.

What is happening?

The autonomic nervous system triggers tiny muscles called arrector pili to contract. This lifts hairs and creates the bumps visible on skin.

Why does it matter?

In furry mammals the response can trap insulating air or make the animal look larger. Humans retain the reflex even with much less body hair.
