Why do onions make your eyes water?

Cutting onion cells releases compounds that form an eye-irritating vapour.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerCutting onion cells releases compounds that form an eye-irritating vapour.

What is happening?

Cutting an onion breaks its cells and lets enzymes react with sulphur-containing compounds. The resulting volatile irritant reaches the eyes and triggers tears.

Why does it matter?

A sharp knife, chilling the onion briefly and good ventilation can reduce irritation. Avoid rubbing your eyes while cutting.
