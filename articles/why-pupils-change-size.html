Why do pupils change size?

The pupil adjusts how much light enters the eye.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerThe pupil adjusts how much light enters the eye.

What is happening?

Muscles in the iris make the pupil smaller in bright light and larger in dim light. Focus, alertness and some medicines can also affect pupil size.

Why does it matter?

Sudden pupil changes with pain or vision loss need urgent medical assessment.
