Why can you smell rain before it falls?

Rain releases earthy scents from soil and plants into the air.

5 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerThat fresh earthy smell after the first rain has a name: petrichor. It comes from a mixture of substances released by dry soil, plants and microbes when rain arrives.

Tiny droplets carry scent

When raindrops hit porous ground, they can trap tiny air bubbles that rise and burst, sending small aerosols into the air. These droplets can carry aromatic compounds. One familiar compound, geosmin, is made by certain soil bacteria and has a strong earthy smell.

Why is the smell stronger after dry weather?

During dry periods, some scent-producing materials can build up on soil and surfaces. The first drops disturb them and carry them into the air. Wind direction, soil type and the amount of rain all affect how strongly you notice the smell.

Try a safe mini experiment

After a light rain, compare the smell near soil, grass and a paved path from a safe sheltered spot. Note which place smells strongest. Do not go out during lightning or flooding.

Words made simpleAerosols are tiny liquid droplets or particles floating in air. Microbes are microscopic living things.
