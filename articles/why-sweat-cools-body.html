How does sweat cool your body?

Evaporating sweat carries heat away from the skin.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerEvaporating sweat carries heat away from the skin.

What is happening?

Changing liquid sweat into vapour requires energy, which is drawn from the skin and nearby tissues. That energy transfer cools the body.

Why does it matter?

High humidity slows evaporation, making heat feel more intense. Drink water and take cooling breaks in hot weather.
