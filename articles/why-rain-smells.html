Why does rain have a distinctive smell?

Raindrops release earthy compounds and oils from the ground into the air.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerRaindrops release earthy compounds and oils from the ground into the air.

What is happening?

The earthy smell after rain is often called petrichor. Raindrops can trap tiny air bubbles that burst and carry aerosol particles, including compounds produced by soil microbes, into the air.

Why does it matter?

The smell varies with soil, plants, weather and how long the ground has been dry. It is chemistry carried by tiny airborne droplets and particles.
