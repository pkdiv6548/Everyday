How do geckos climb smooth walls?

Microscopic structures on their toes create strong surface contact.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerMicroscopic structures on their toes create strong surface contact.

What is happening?

Gecko toes have millions of tiny hair-like structures called setae. Their tips get close enough for weak intermolecular attractions to add up to strong grip.

Why does it matter?

The grip is dry and reversible, letting geckos attach and detach their feet quickly. Researchers study the design for reusable adhesives.
