Why do stars have different colours?

A star’s surface temperature strongly influences its colour.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerA star’s surface temperature strongly influences its colour.

What is happening?

Hotter stars tend to look blue or white, while cooler stars often appear orange or red. The colour is related to the spectrum of light the star emits.

Why does it matter?

Dust, Earth’s atmosphere and camera settings can also affect the colour seen in a photograph.
