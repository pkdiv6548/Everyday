Why are most leaves green?

Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light more strongly than green light.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerChlorophyll absorbs red and blue light more strongly than green light.

What is happening?

Chlorophyll helps plants capture light energy for photosynthesis. Green wavelengths are reflected or transmitted more than some other colours, so leaves often look green to us.

Why does it matter?

Other pigments can dominate in young leaves or in autumn, when chlorophyll breaks down.
