Why is space silent?

Sound needs a material medium, and space is almost a vacuum.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerSound needs a material medium, and space is almost a vacuum.

What is happening?

Sound travels as vibrations passed between particles. Space has too few particles to carry ordinary sound waves from one place to another.

Why does it matter?

Astronauts communicate using radio waves, which can travel through a vacuum even though ordinary sound cannot.
