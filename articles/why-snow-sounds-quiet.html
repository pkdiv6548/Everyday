Why can fresh snow make the world quieter?

Porous fresh snow absorbs some sound instead of reflecting it strongly.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerPorous fresh snow absorbs some sound instead of reflecting it strongly.

What is happening?

Fresh snow contains many tiny air pockets. Sound entering those pores loses energy through friction and heat, so some echoes and high-frequency sounds become less noticeable.

Why does it matter?

The effect depends on snow depth, density, wind and the surrounding landscape.
