Why do soap bubbles show rainbow colours?

Light reflects from both sides of a very thin soap film.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerLight reflects from both sides of a very thin soap film.

What is happening?

Reflected light waves overlap. Depending on film thickness, some colours reinforce while others cancel—a process called thin-film interference.

Why does it matter?

As water drains and film thickness changes, the colours shift across the bubble.
