Why does iron rust?

Iron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form rust.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerIron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form rust.

What is happening?

Rust forms through chemical reactions involving iron, oxygen and water. Salts can speed up corrosion by helping electrical charge move through the moisture.

Why does it matter?

Paint, oil and protective coatings slow rust by separating iron from air and water.
