How does bread dough rise?

Yeast releases carbon dioxide that gets trapped in the dough.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerYeast releases carbon dioxide that gets trapped in the dough.

What is happening?

Yeast feeds on sugars in dough and produces carbon dioxide and other compounds. The stretchy gluten network traps the gas, expanding the dough.

Why does it matter?

Temperature, time, hydration and yeast activity affect the rise. Too much heat can damage yeast, while too little slows fermentation.
