Why do shadows change length?

A shadow changes as the light source’s angle changes.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerA shadow changes as the light source’s angle changes.

What is happening?

When the Sun is low, light reaches objects at a shallow angle and shadows stretch. Near midday, the Sun is higher and shadows are usually shorter.

Why does it matter?

Shadow direction also changes during the day because Earth rotates, making a simple sundial possible.
