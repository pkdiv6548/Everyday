What makes popcorn pop?

Steam pressure builds inside a hard kernel until its shell bursts.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerSteam pressure builds inside a hard kernel until its shell bursts.

What is happening?

A popcorn kernel contains a little water inside a tough hull. Heating turns the water to steam; pressure builds until the hull breaks and the starchy interior expands rapidly.

Why does it matter?

The expanded starch cools into the fluffy shape we eat. Kernels that are too dry or have damaged hulls may not pop well.
