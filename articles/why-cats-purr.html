Why do cats purr?

Cats purr through rhythmic activity in the muscles around the voice box.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerCats purr through rhythmic activity in the muscles around the voice box.

What is happening?

A cat’s brain sends repeated signals to muscles around the larynx. As the cat breathes in and out, the vocal folds vibrate and create a purr.

Why does it matter?

Cats may purr when relaxed, but also in stressful situations. The sound alone does not always tell you how a cat feels.
