Why does popcorn suddenly pop?

A tiny drop of water turns a hard kernel into a fluffy snack.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerA popcorn kernel is a tiny pressure cooker. Heat, water and a tough outer shell work together until the shell can no longer hold the pressure.

What is hiding inside the kernel?

Inside each kernel is a little water and starch. When the kernel heats up, the water becomes steam. Steam takes up more space, so pressure rises inside the hard shell.

Why does it turn fluffy?

At the right temperature, the shell breaks. The hot starch expands quickly and cools into the airy, crunchy shape we eat. Not every kernel pops: some may have a cracked shell or too little moisture.

Try a safe mini experiment

Try this: watch an adult prepare popcorn according to the packet instructions. Notice that the kernels stay quiet for a while, then pop quickly. Never heat sealed containers or handle hot oil yourself.

Words made simpleA good experiment changes one thing at a time, observes carefully and compares results. Never taste unknown materials or use dangerous heat, chemicals or electricity.
