Why does salt help melt ice?

Salt lowers water’s freezing point when it dissolves in liquid water.

4 MIN READEVERYDAY ODDITIES
The short answerSalt lowers water’s freezing point when it dissolves in liquid water.

What is happening?

Salt ions mix with liquid water and make it harder for water molecules to organise into an ice crystal at 0°C. This can melt ice when conditions are warm enough.

Why does it matter?

Salt becomes less effective in very cold weather, and salty runoff can harm plants and freshwater ecosystems.
