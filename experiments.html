SMALL TESTS, BIG AHA MOMENTS

Science is better hands-on.

Try these low-cost observations at home. Ask a question, make a prediction, and notice what changes. Adult supervision is recommended for children.

01 · LIGHT

Make a pencil look bent

You need: a clear glass, water and a pencil. Place the pencil in the empty glass, then add water and observe it from the side.

  1. Look at the pencil before adding water.
  2. Pour water into the glass slowly.
  3. Compare how the pencil appears through air and water.
02 · WATER

Make a water drop dome

You need: a coin, dropper or teaspoon, and water. Add drops carefully and count how many fit before spilling.

  1. Place the coin on a flat surface.
  2. Add one small drop at a time.
  3. Watch the curved dome and record your count.
03 · OBSERVATION

Track a shadow

You need: chalk or paper, a sunny spot and a safe fixed object. Mark the shadow at different times.

  1. Mark the tip of the shadow in the morning.
  2. Repeat later from the same position.
  3. Compare the direction and length.
